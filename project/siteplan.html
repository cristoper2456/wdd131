Site Plan: Explora RD

Site Name

Explora RD

This name means "Explore DR" in Spanish, representing a tourism guide dedicated to showcasing the beaches, culture, and destinations of the Dominican Republic.

Site Purpose

The site provides visitors with information about popular destinations, beaches, and activities in the Dominican Republic, along with a contact form for booking tours or requesting travel information.

Scenarios

  • What are the best beaches to visit in the Dominican Republic?
  • How can I contact the company to book a tour?

Color Schema

  • Red (#c8102e) — headers, buttons, accents
  • Blue (#002d62) — navigation, footer background
  • White (#ffffff) — background, text on dark sections

Typography

Poppins — used for headings

Roboto — used for body text
